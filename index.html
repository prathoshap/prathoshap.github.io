About

Principled machine learning — reliable learning and correction

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical Communication Engineering at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, where I lead the Representation Learning Lab (RepLab). My research focuses on how machine learning systems can learn and be corrected reliably when data is scarce, noisy, or biased.

Before IISc, I was an Assistant Professor at IIT Delhi (2017–2021) and held research positions at Xerox Research Centre India and Philips Innovation Labs. I received my Ph.D. from IISc in 2015 and B.E. from SJCE Mysore in 2011. I also hold a concurrent appointment as Visiting Faculty at the Centre for Brain Research (CBR), IISc.

I co-founded LatentForce.ai (IISc-FSID incubated, USD 1.7M seed funding) and Cogniable, an autism intervention platform acquired by Frontera Inc., USA in 2024. I serve on the Committee for Responsible AI, Government of Karnataka.

Research

Research Areas

IISc, September 2021 – present.

Data-Efficient & Trustworthy Learning

Learning reliably from noisy or scarce data using submodular selection, DPP-based attribution, and hypothesis testing. Applied to LLM fine-tuning, drug response prediction, and anomaly detection.

WISER (ICML 2024) · f-INE (ICLR 2026) · Trustworthy LLMs (ICLR 2026) · Text Attribution (TMLR 2026)

Machine Unlearning

Concept removal with formal guarantees across GANs, diffusion models, and discriminative classifiers. Covers selective forgetting and black-box output filtering.

Adapt-then-Unlearn (TMLR 2025) · FAST (IEEE Trans. AI 2024) · Partially Blinded Unlearning (AAAI 2025) · UAI 2025

Symmetry Discovery & Equivariant Learning

Recovering symmetry groups — discrete and continuous Lie groups — directly from data, with invariance built into the architecture.

AISTATS 2023 · AISTATS 2024 · AISTATS 2026 · ICML 2026

Neural Operators & Scientific ML

Neural operators in the complex Fourier domain for improved OOD generalisation. Applied to PDE solving and real-time air quality forecasting (R² > 0.95).

CoNO (APL ML 2025) · Latent Mamba Operator (ICML 2025) · CoNOAir (npj Clean Air 2025) · CoDBench (Digital Discovery 2024)

Generative AI for Medicine

Reducing annotation requirements in medical image analysis — from bounding-box supervision down to fully self-supervised diffusion-based methods.

GenSelfDiff-HIS (IEEE TMI 2024) · DeGPR (CVPR 2023) · BoxCell (Scientific Reports 2025) · LangDAug (ICML 2025)
